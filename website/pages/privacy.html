Privacy

Short version: your videos, projects and API keys stay on your PC. This website keeps only what it needs to remind you about updates.

What this website stores

WhatWhyHow
UsernameTo sign you inAs you typed it
PasswordTo sign you inOnly a salted one-way scrypt hash — nobody, including the site owner, can read your password
Email (only if you add one)Only to send a reset code when you ask for oneEncrypted (AES-256-GCM); never shown to anyone, never used for anything else, never shared
Latest downloaded version + dateTo tell you when a newer version existsUpdated each time you download
Sign-in sessionTo keep you signed in (30 days)One cookie; the site stores only a hash of it
Sign-in failure counterTo lock an account for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwordsA number, reset after a good sign-in

What it never stores

  • Your footage, projects, exports, voices or API keys
  • IP addresses, device or browser fingerprints, location
  • Analytics, tracking pixels, advertising cookies — there are none

What the site owner can see

Anonymous counts: how many accounts exist, how many are new, and which version people last downloaded — by user number only. Never your password (only a hash exists) and never your email.

The editor on your PC

  • It runs at http://localhost:8765 — only your own browser can open it.
  • It talks to the internet only for: Setup downloads; your own AI requests to Anthropic / ElevenLabs with your keys (their privacy terms apply to what you send them); reference links you paste into the AI Editor; and one anonymous update check a day (it sends nothing about you; turn it off with ANKAR_UPDATE_CHECK=0).
  • API keys are encrypted on your PC, protected by Windows and by your own password.

Your choices

  • See your data: Account ▸ Show my data.
  • Remove your email: Account ▸ Remove email.
  • Delete everything: Account ▸ Delete account — your row is erased immediately.

Where it's hosted

The website runs on Vercel; its small database is Neon Postgres. Downloads come from GitHub Releases. Those providers process web requests to deliver the site.
