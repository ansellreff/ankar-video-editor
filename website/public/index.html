Private by design

  • Your videos never leave your PC. The editor runs at localhost, only for you.
  • Your API keys stay encrypted on your PC, behind your own password — never shown again, never uploaded.
  • This website stores almost nothing: your username, a one-way hash of your password, your email only if you add one (encrypted, only for "forgot password"), and which version you last downloaded.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no IP addresses. You can see everything stored about you and delete your account anytime.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes — the editor is free and open source (MIT). The optional AI features use your own Claude and/or ElevenLabs keys, which those companies bill per use. Captions, editing and export run on your PC for free.

Why do I need an account to download?

Only to remind you when a new version is out: the site remembers your username and the version you downloaded. No email needed (add one only if you want "forgot password"). The source code is also on GitHub without any account.

Will the AI change my video on its own?

No. Every change and every paid action is shown as a proposal first. Nothing happens until you press Approve — that rule is enforced in the code, not just asked of the AI.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 (1803+) or 11, 64-bit, 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), a graphics chip with OpenGL 3.3 (nearly every PC from the last 10 years), Edge or Chrome, and about 3 GB of disk plus your footage.

Mac or Linux?

Not yet — the one-click setup is Windows-only for now. Developers can run it from source on other systems.
